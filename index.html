03 / Oopbuy shopping guide

A better list starts with better notes.

A long product list becomes useful when each row helps answer a purchasing question. Keep your Oopbuy finds organized around fit, evidence, and total cost.

  1. Pick one category and one purpose.

    Decide whether you need an everyday sneaker, a layering piece, or an accessory. A focused shortlist is easier to compare.

  2. Record the exact item and variant.

    Save the listing URL, model or item name, color, selected size, seller, and the date you checked the information.

  3. Review measurements and photographs.

    Compare measurements with something you already own. Where available, inspect product-specific photographs and note unresolved details.

  4. Calculate before committing.

    Separate the item price from shipping, service charges, currency conversion, and any applicable import costs. Confirm the return terms.

05 / Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet

Fit is more useful than a size label.

For hoodies, sweaters, T-shirts, and jackets, compare chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length, and garment length with a piece you already wear. Check whether a chart shows body measurements or garment measurements.

For pants and shorts, record the waist, rise, inseam, and leg opening. For sets and jerseys, check each piece separately and note whether the intended fit is close, regular, or relaxed.

Use your sheet to preserve what the listing actually says. When measurements or materials are missing, mark them as unconfirmed rather than filling the gap with a guess.

Before a find becomes a purchase

  • Does the selected variant match the photographs you reviewed?
  • Are the measurements and units clear?
  • Is the price shown in the currency you expect?
  • Have you checked shipping availability for your destination?
  • Are return eligibility and costs explained?
  • Have you separated confirmed facts from assumptions?
06 / Clear answers

Oopbuy spreadsheet questions, answered.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-organizing sheet used to collect shopping links and comparison notes. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy of each entry, including the selected variant, size, price, and seller information.

Is this a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and shopping guide. It links to CAFANS categories and does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or a live Oopbuy product feed.

How should I compare two product entries?

Compare the same size and variant, then review measurements, materials, photographs, seller terms, and the estimated total cost. A lower listed item price alone does not establish better value.

Do brand photographs prove authenticity or availability?

No. Editorial brand photographs do not verify an item offered by a separate seller, prove authenticity, or confirm availability. Check the destination listing and request appropriate product-specific evidence.

Are shipping costs and delivery times included?

This guide does not provide shipping quotations or delivery promises. Confirm the destination, parcel weight and dimensions, shipping method, applicable taxes, and any service charges before paying.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide. It is not an official Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, or Hermès website. Shopping links lead to CAFANS.
